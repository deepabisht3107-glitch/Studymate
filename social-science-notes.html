Welcome to your Social Science notes. Learn important concepts from History, Geography, Civics and Economics.

1. 🏛️ History

What is History?

History is the study of events, people, societies and civilizations of the past.

Sources of History

Historians use different sources to learn about the past.

  • Inscriptions
  • Coins
  • Buildings and monuments
  • Books and manuscripts
  • Paintings and other objects

Why Do We Study History?

  • To understand the past.
  • To learn about different civilizations.
  • To understand how societies changed.
  • To learn from events of the past.

Important Historical Terms

  • Empire: A group of territories ruled by one authority.
  • Dynasty: A series of rulers belonging to the same family.
  • Monument: A structure built to remember a person or event.
  • Archaeology: The study of the human past through remains and objects.

2. 🌏 Geography

What is Geography?

Geography is the study of the Earth, its physical features, people, places and environment.

Natural Resources

Natural resources are materials and substances obtained from nature that are useful to humans.

Examples:

  • Water
  • Air
  • Soil
  • Forests
  • Minerals

Renewable Resources

Renewable resources can be naturally replaced or renewed.

Examples: Sunlight, wind and water.

Non-Renewable Resources

Non-renewable resources are limited and take a very long time to form.

Examples: Coal, petroleum and natural gas.

Landforms

Landforms are natural features found on the Earth's surface.

  • Mountains
  • Plateaus
  • Plains
  • Deserts
  • Valleys

Climate

Climate is the usual pattern of weather of a place over a long period of time.

3. 🏛️ Civics

What is Civics?

Civics is the study of government, citizenship, rights and responsibilities.

Democracy

Democracy is a system of government in which people participate in choosing their representatives.

Constitution

A Constitution is a set of basic rules and principles according to which a country is governed.

Rights

Rights are freedoms and protections given to people by law.

Duties

Duties are responsibilities that citizens are expected to perform.

Government

A government makes and implements rules and policies and provides public services.

Levels of Government in India

  • Central Government
  • State Government
  • Local Government

Equality

Equality means that people should be treated fairly and have equal rights under the law.

4. 💰 Economics

What is Economics?

Economics is the study of how people use resources to satisfy their needs and wants.

Needs

Needs are things that are necessary for living.

Examples: Food, water, shelter and clothing.

Wants

Wants are things that people would like to have but are not essential for survival.

Examples: Video games, expensive gadgets and luxury items.

Goods

Goods are physical things that satisfy human needs or wants.

Examples: Books, clothes, food and furniture.

Services

Services are activities performed for people.

Examples: Teaching, banking, transportation and healthcare.

Production

Production is the process of making goods or providing services.

Consumers

Consumers are people who buy or use goods and services.

5. 🌱 Environment and Society

Environment

The environment includes everything around us, including living and non-living things.

Human Activities and Environment

Human activities such as cutting forests, pollution and excessive use of natural resources can affect the environment.

How Can We Protect the Environment?

  • Plant more trees.
  • Save water.
  • Reduce pollution.
  • Reuse and recycle materials.
  • Save electricity.
  • Protect wildlife.

⭐ Quick Revision

  • History studies the events and people of the past.
  • Geography studies the Earth, places, people and environment.
  • Civics studies government, citizenship, rights and duties.
  • Economics studies needs, wants, resources, goods and services.
  • Renewable resources can be naturally renewed.
  • Non-renewable resources are limited.
  • Democracy involves people in choosing their representatives.
  • Consumers buy or use goods and services.
  • We should protect and conserve natural resources.
