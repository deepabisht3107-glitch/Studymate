📐 Mathematics Notes

Welcome to your Mathematics notes. Learn important concepts with simple explanations and examples.

1. 🔢 Number System

Natural Numbers

Natural numbers are counting numbers starting from 1.

Examples: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6...

Whole Numbers

Whole numbers include 0 and all natural numbers.

Examples: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5...

Integers

Integers include positive numbers, negative numbers and zero.

Examples: -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3

Rational Numbers

A rational number can be written in the form p/q, where q is not zero.

Examples: 1/2, 3/4, -5/6, 7/2

Important Rules

  • Any number multiplied by 0 is 0.
  • Any number multiplied by 1 remains the same.
  • Division by zero is not possible.

Example

3 + 5 = 8

-2 + 5 = 3

2. ✏️ Algebra

What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics in which letters and symbols are used to represent numbers.

Variable

A variable is a letter whose value can change.

Examples: x, y, a, b

Constant

A constant is a number whose value does not change.

Examples: 5, 10, 25

Coefficient

A coefficient is the number multiplied by a variable.

In 5x, 5 is the coefficient of x.

Algebraic Expression

An algebraic expression contains numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

Examples:

  • x + 5
  • 3x + 2
  • 5a - 7
  • 2x + 3y

Simple Equation

An equation contains an equal sign (=).

Example:

x + 5 = 10

Subtract 5 from both sides:

x = 5

3. 📐 Geometry

Point

A point represents an exact position.

Line

A line extends endlessly in both directions.

Line Segment

A line segment has two fixed endpoints.

Ray

A ray has one fixed endpoint and extends endlessly in one direction.

Types of Angles

  • Acute Angle: Less than 90°
  • Right Angle: Exactly 90°
  • Obtuse Angle: More than 90° but less than 180°
  • Straight Angle: Exactly 180°
  • Reflex Angle: More than 180° but less than 360°

Types of Triangles

  • Equilateral: All three sides are equal.
  • Isosceles: Two sides are equal.
  • Scalene: All three sides are different.

Important Rule

The sum of the three angles of a triangle is always 180°.

Example

180° - 60° - 70° = 50°

4. 📏 Mensuration

What is Mensuration?

Mensuration is the study of measurement of shapes, including length, perimeter, area and volume.

Perimeter

Perimeter is the total distance around a closed shape.

Square

Perimeter = 4 × side

Area = side × side

Rectangle

Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth)

Area = length × breadth

Example

Length = 10 cm
Breadth = 5 cm

Area = 10 × 5 = 50 cm²

Volume

Volume tells us how much space a three-dimensional object occupies.

Volume of cuboid = length × breadth × height

5. 📊 Data Handling

What is Data?

Data is a collection of information or numbers.

Example: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50

Mean

Mean is also called average.

Mean = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

Example

Data: 10, 20, 30

Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 = 60

Number of values = 3

Mean = 60 ÷ 3 = 20

Median

Median is the middle value when numbers are arranged in order.

Example: 10, 20, 30

Median = 20

Mode

Mode is the value that occurs most frequently.

Example: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5

Mode = 3

Range

Range = Highest value - Lowest value

Example: 5, 10, 15, 20

Range = 20 - 5 = 15

⭐ Quick Revision

  • Natural numbers start from 1.
  • Whole numbers start from 0.
  • Integers include positive and negative numbers.
  • An equation contains an equal sign.
  • Sum of angles of a triangle = 180°.
  • Area of rectangle = length × breadth.
  • Mean = Sum of values ÷ Number of values.
  • Range = Highest value - Lowest value.
