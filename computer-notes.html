Welcome to your English notes. Learn grammar, vocabulary and writing skills with simple examples.

1. 📚 Grammar

What is Grammar?

Grammar is the set of rules that helps us use words and sentences correctly.

Sentence

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete meaning.

Example: Ansh is reading a book.

Subject and Predicate

The subject tells us who or what the sentence is about.

The predicate tells us something about the subject.

Example:

Ansh | is playing cricket.

Subject = Ansh

Predicate = is playing cricket

2. 🔤 Parts of Speech

Parts of speech are categories of words based on the work they do in a sentence.

1. Noun

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples: Ansh, India, school, dog, honesty

2. Pronoun

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Examples: he, she, it, they, we, you

Example: Ansh is a student. He studies every day.

3. Verb

A verb shows an action or state.

Examples: run, eat, write, play, is, are

4. Adjective

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun.

Examples: beautiful, tall, clever, red

Example: She has a beautiful dress.

5. Adverb

An adverb describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Examples: quickly, slowly, carefully, loudly

Example: He runs quickly.

6. Preposition

A preposition shows the relationship between words.

Examples: in, on, under, near, beside, between

Example: The book is on the table.

7. Conjunction

A conjunction joins words or sentences.

Examples: and, but, because, or, so

Example: I wanted to play, but it was raining.

8. Interjection

An interjection expresses a sudden feeling or emotion.

Examples: Wow!, Oh!, Hurrah!, Alas!

3. ⏰ Tenses

What are Tenses?

Tense tells us when an action happens.

Present Tense

Present tense is used for actions that happen now or regularly.

Example: I play cricket.

Past Tense

Past tense is used for actions that happened before.

Example: I played cricket yesterday.

Future Tense

Future tense is used for actions that will happen later.

Example: I will play cricket tomorrow.

Easy Trick

  • Present → happening now or regularly
  • Past → already happened
  • Future → will happen later

4. 📝 Articles

Articles are words used before nouns.

A

Use a before a singular noun that begins with a consonant sound.

Examples: a book, a dog, a school

An

Use an before a singular noun that begins with a vowel sound.

Examples: an apple, an orange, an umbrella

The

Use the when talking about a specific person, place or thing.

Examples: the Sun, the Taj Mahal, the book on the table

5. 🧠 Vocabulary

Synonyms

Synonyms are words that have the same or similar meanings.

  • Happy → Joyful
  • Big → Large
  • Small → Tiny
  • Fast → Quick

Antonyms

Antonyms are words with opposite meanings.

  • Hot → Cold
  • Big → Small
  • Happy → Sad
  • Early → Late

Homophones

Homophones are words that sound the same but have different meanings or spellings.

  • See → Sea
  • Right → Write
  • Son → Sun
  • Two → Too

6. ✍️ Writing Skills

Paragraph Writing

A good paragraph should have a clear topic, supporting details and a proper ending.

Letter Writing

A letter usually contains an address, date, greeting, main message and closing.

Notice Writing

A notice gives important information about an event, announcement or activity.

Story Writing

A story usually has characters, a setting, events and an ending.

Writing Tips

  • Use simple and clear sentences.
  • Use correct punctuation.
  • Check spelling.
  • Use paragraphs where needed.
  • Read your answer once after writing.

7. ❗ Punctuation

Punctuation marks help make sentences clear and easy to understand.

  • Full stop (.) → Used at the end of a statement.
  • Question mark (?) → Used at the end of a question.
  • Exclamation mark (!) → Shows strong feeling.
  • Comma (,) → Shows a short pause or separates items.
  • Apostrophe (') → Shows possession or contraction.

Examples

Where are you?

What a beautiful day!

I bought apples, bananas and oranges.

This is Ansh's book.

  • Present tense describes present or regular actions.
  • Past tense describes completed actions.
