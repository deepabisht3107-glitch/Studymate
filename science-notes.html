Welcome to your Science notes. Learn important concepts with simple explanations and examples.

1. ⚛️ Physics

What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, force and many natural phenomena.

Force

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion or shape of an object.

Examples:

  • Pushing a door
  • Pulling a drawer
  • Kicking a football

Types of Force

  • Contact Force: Force that acts when objects are touching.
  • Non-contact Force: Force that acts without touching.

Examples of Non-contact Forces

  • Gravitational force
  • Magnetic force
  • Electrostatic force

Friction

Friction is a force that opposes the motion of an object.

Example: A ball rolling on the ground eventually stops because of friction.

2. 🧪 Chemistry

What is Chemistry?

Chemistry is the branch of science that studies substances, their properties and the changes they undergo.

Matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.

States of Matter

  • Solid: Has a fixed shape and fixed volume.
  • Liquid: Has a fixed volume but no fixed shape.
  • Gas: Has no fixed shape and no fixed volume.

Examples

  • Solid → Ice
  • Liquid → Water
  • Gas → Water vapour

Physical Change

A physical change changes the form or state of a substance without forming a new substance.

Example: Melting of ice.

Chemical Change

A chemical change produces one or more new substances.

Example: Rusting of iron.

3. 🧬 Biology

What is Biology?

Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms.

Living Organisms

Living organisms are things that show characteristics of life.

Examples: Humans, animals, plants and microorganisms.

Characteristics of Living Things

  • They need food.
  • They breathe or carry out respiration.
  • They grow.
  • They reproduce.
  • They respond to their surroundings.
  • They eventually die.

Cell

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Plant Cell

Plant cells have structures such as the cell wall, cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus and chloroplasts.

Animal Cell

Animal cells have structures such as the cell membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus.

4. 🌱 Environment

What is Environment?

The environment includes all living and non-living things around us.

Components of Environment

  • Biotic: Living things such as plants, animals and humans.
  • Abiotic: Non-living things such as air, water, soil and sunlight.

Food Chain

A food chain shows how energy passes from one organism to another through food.

Example:

Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle

Pollution

Pollution is the harmful change in our environment caused by unwanted substances.

Types of Pollution

  • Air pollution
  • Water pollution
  • Soil pollution
  • Noise pollution

How Can We Protect the Environment?

  • Plant more trees.
  • Save water.
  • Reduce plastic waste.
  • Reuse and recycle materials.
  • Keep our surroundings clean.
